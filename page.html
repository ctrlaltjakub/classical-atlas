Classical music on Spotify, organized into one playlist per composer for listening and exploration.

Playlist order

  1. Start with the most important works. Between similarly important genres, the shorter section comes first.
  2. Keep movements together. Usually one recording per work; prefer a strong Karajan or Argerich reading, never an isolated extra movement.
  3. Include significant orchestrations on their own merits. Prefer ballet suites; put operas last.

Selections target the German catalogue. Availability and recording gaps vary.
