Classical playlists

Methodology

The playlists start with the genres most important to each composer. Within each genre, the most important pieces come first. When two genres are similarly important, the shorter section comes first.

The most important recordings with acceptable sound quality were chosen. Movements of a work stay together. The releases were available in Germany when selected.

Historically important orchestral versions sit alongside other orchestral works, including when someone else made the arrangement. A ballet is usually represented by its concert suite: a selection of dances from the score, rather than the entire staged work. Operas come after the other genres because a single opera can occupy dozens of tracks.
